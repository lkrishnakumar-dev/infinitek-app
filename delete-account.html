Account

Delete Account

You can delete your account directly from supported InfiniteK apps, including Strivio.

In-app deletion

  1. Open the app.
  2. Go to Profile or Settings.
  3. Select Account or Privacy options.
  4. Choose Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Request by email

If you cannot access the app, email support@infinitek.app from the email associated with your account.

What may be deleted

Account profile, user-owned app data, synced fitness logs, nutrition logs, workout records, progress summaries, and related cloud data.

What may be retained

We may retain limited records where required for security, legal, fraud prevention, or platform subscription history.
