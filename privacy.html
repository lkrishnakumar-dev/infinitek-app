Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This policy explains how InfiniteK handles information across our websites, applications, and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect account details, app activity, device information, support messages, and product usage data needed to provide and improve InfiniteK products. Health, fitness, and nutrition information is handled with additional care and used to power the features you choose to use.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate our apps, sync data across devices, personalize product experiences, provide support, improve reliability, prevent abuse, and develop new features.

Privacy First

InfiniteK designs products to limit unnecessary data collection. We do not sell personal information. Where third-party services are used for hosting, analytics, authentication, payments, notifications, or cloud sync, they are used to support product functionality.

Your Choices

You can request account deletion, contact support, or manage app permissions through your device settings. For account and data requests, email support@infinitek.app.

Updates

We may update this policy as our products evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
