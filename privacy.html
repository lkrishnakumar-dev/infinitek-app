Privacy

Privacy Policy

InfiniteK builds privacy-first applications. This page explains the types of information our apps may collect, how it is used, and how users can contact us.

Information We Collect

Our apps may collect account information, app activity, fitness and nutrition logs, workout data, health summaries when authorized, and diagnostic information required to operate and improve the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide app functionality, sync user data across devices, personalize insights, improve reliability, and provide support.

Health Data

Health-related data is used only to provide app features requested by the user. Users can control access through device permissions and app settings.

Data Security

We use account-based access controls and cloud services designed to protect user information. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@infinitek.app.
